Questions

Good to know

Why does Gmail need an extra step for my photo?

Gmail removes images that are packed inside a signature. So for Gmail, Signoff copies your signature with a "Photo" marker, you download the photo it made for you, and you add it with Gmail's own Insert image button. Gmail then stores it for you. If your photo already lives at a public link, paste that link in step 2 and you can skip all of this.

Why do my icons turn into letters in Gmail?

Icons are small images, and Gmail would need each one added by hand. Letters are plain text, so they come through in every inbox. Outlook and Apple Mail keep the icons.

Will it look the same for everyone?

Close to it. Most apps show the card as you see it here. Classic Outlook for Windows ignores rounded corners and gradients, so it shows square corners and a flat color. Your text, links and photo still come through.

Can I come back and edit it later?

Nothing is saved, on purpose. Download the HTML file before you leave: it opens in any browser, and you can copy your signature from it again.

Does it work on my phone?

You can build it on your phone. To use it in iPhone Mail, copy it there and paste it under Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature. The Gmail phone app only takes plain text, so use Copy plain text for it.
